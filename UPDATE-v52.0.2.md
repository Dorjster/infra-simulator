# Infra Simulator 52.0.2 — Global Defensive: Nuke vents and B glass

Global Defensive only. A small fix update on top of 52.0.1.

## Nuke vents
- Jumping in the vents no longer sends you through the roof and out of the map. A small step along one wall left your head touching the vent roof, and the game ignored a roof your head was already in. Some low rooms on Ancient and Inferno had the same problem, and they are fixed too. All 8 maps were checked with jumps under every low ceiling.
- The vent board is there and blocks until it is broken, as before. Bots break it when they chase someone through the vents, as in CS2.

## Nuke B glass
- Every piece of glass on B breaks now: the 44 floor windows, the floor panels set in metal (they did not break before) and the two Observation windows. Bullets, knives, grenades and bots' shots all break it.
- B's floor still holds with all the glass broken. Every LAN player sees the same broken glass, and it resets each round.
