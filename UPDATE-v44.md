# v44: Global Defensive, the sound, buy menu and maps update

## Maps
- Real height on the classic maps: raised floors, stairs and roofed tunnels.
  - **Dune (Dust II):** the upper tunnels are roofed and lead to a raised B. The stairs go down into the roofed lower tunnels and out to mid. A sits up the ramps from long, short and the CT side.
  - **Temple (Ancient):** the jungle stairs climb to a raised A beside the pillared temple. The donut, the cave, the lamproom and the tunnel to T spawn are roofed. B stands raised over short, long and the ruins ramp.
  - **Canal (Anubis):** the bridge and top mid stand over the canal. The roofed palace climbs to B, and the roofed heaven tunnel climbs from the CT spawn to A.
  - **Old City (Inferno):** banana climbs to a raised B. The T apartments are upstairs under a roof, and boiler, the underpass and the library are roofed.
  - **Plaza (Mirage):** the T and B apartments are upstairs, and the B stairs go down to B. The connector, jungle, window room, market and palace are covered.
  - **Hamlet (Dust):** a roofed underpass leads to the stairs up to B, and A sits up the east lane.
  - **Depot (Nuke):** the A building is raised and indoors, reached by the hut, lockers, main and the long ramp room from B.
  - **Park (Overpass):** the halls and the toilets are roofed, and the ladder room stands raised.
- Tunnels and halls are dim inside, with their own walls, floor and timbered ceiling.
- More props on every map: barrels, carts, pallets, planters, benches, trees and pillars.
- The radar shows tunnels hatched, stairs as rungs, and higher floors lighter.
- Bots walk the stairs and raised floors, and still plant and defuse on every map.

## Sound
- Every gun has its own real recording, heard close up and from afar. The far recording fades in with distance.
- Recorded grenade, bomb, flashbang, glass, fire, bounce, shell-casing, bullet-impact and body-hit sounds.
- All 102 sound files are the enhanced versions.

## Buy menu (CS2 style)
- Six columns: Pistols, SMGs, Heavy, Rifles, Equipment and Grenades.
- Each item is a card with a picture of the gun's own model, its price and an Owned tag.
- Buy by number keys: 1–6 picks a column, then 1–9 buys.
- A loadout panel and the gun's info card sit at the bottom.
- **Undo buy:** anything bought this round and still unused can be sold back for its full price while buy time lasts. Right-click it, click its ↺ badge, or press Backspace to undo the last purchase. A gun brings back the one it replaced.

## Kill feed (CS2 style)
- Shows a white silhouette of the weapon.
- Marks: blind, no-scope, airborne, through smoke, wallbang, head shot, distance for long kills, and assists.

## Effects
- **HE grenade:** a billowing fireball, sparks, dark smoke, dust and a shock ring.
- **Bomb:** a bigger version of the same.
- **Molotov:** flickering, varied flames with rising embers and smoke.
- **Smoke:** grows without hard edges.

## Weapons
- The USP-S, Five-SeveN and Desert Eagle now have their own models (they had fallen back to the Glock's).
- Wider CS2-style sprays on the automatic guns.

## LAN
- The ping shown is the true network round trip, measured without the page's own frame time.
- Positions reach other players sooner: they are forwarded as soon as they arrive, and sent 30 times a second in matches.

## Fixes
- On Tab, the map no longer covers the scoreboard.
- Toasts in a match moved to the left, off the kill feed.
