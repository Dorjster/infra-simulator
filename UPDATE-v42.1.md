# v42.1: Global Defensive, CS2 feel update

## Grenades (CS2 throw mechanics)
- A click throws as soon as the pin is out. Holding waits until you let go. You can press during the draw.
- Left is a long throw, right a short underhand, both a medium throw.
- CS2 grenade physics:
  - A level aim throws slightly upward.
  - Weaker throws start lower and fly slower.
  - Your running and jumping speed carries into the throw.
- After a throw you switch back to your last weapon. If you have another grenade of the same kind, it comes up.

## Recoil (CS:GO style)
- The crosshair stays fixed at the centre. The view rises about half as much as the bullets do, so a spray climbs above the crosshair and you pull down to control it. The first bullet is still exact.
- Each shot gives a small kick to the view. The dynamic crosshair opens while you spray.

## Snipers
- AWP and SSG 08: after each shot the rifle rolls up to work the bolt and settles exactly when you can fire again.
- Flick shots land:
  - Fast mouse movements are no longer cut off.
  - The mouse is read raw, without operating system acceleration.
  - The kick wears off before the next shot is ready.

## Sound
- Fuller gunshots: a deeper boom, a supersonic crack for rifles and snipers, and the gun's mechanical click on your own shots.
- Shots and explosions echo outdoors. Distant shots are muffled and echo off the walls, so you can hear how far away they are.
- A thud for body hits and the metallic helmet "dink" for headshots.
