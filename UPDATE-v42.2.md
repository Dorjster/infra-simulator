# v42.2: Global Defensive fixes

## Fixes
- Walls are solid again: smoke, fire, bullet holes and lamp glows no longer show through walls or float off them. Your gun still never clips into a wall you stand against.
- Reloads work like CS2: the new magazine counts only once it is seated, near the end of the reload. Switching weapons before that cancels the reload and keeps your old magazine.
- Opening the buy menu (or any menu) in mid-air no longer freezes you there. You land and slow down normally.
- Long callout signs (like WAREHOUSE) fit on the wall instead of being cut off.

## Recoil
- Every rifle and SMG has its own CS2-style spray:
  - AK-47: climbs hard, then left, right, left.
  - M4A4: climbs, then right, then left.
  - MAC-10: drifts strongly left.
  - The others each have their own shape.

## Counter-Strike 1.6 player models
- The desktop app finds CS 1.6 in your own Steam install (Windows, Mac or Linux) and loads its Terrorist and Counter-Terrorist models automatically.
- Manual import still works under Settings → Arena characters.
- The models are never included in the download, because they belong to Valve. Each player sees them only on a computer that has them.
