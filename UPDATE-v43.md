# v43: Global Defensive, the classic maps update

## Maps
- Eight Defuse maps built on the classic CS flows, with our own layouts and names: Dune (Dust II), Old City (Inferno), Plaza (Mirage), Hamlet (Dust), Depot (Nuke, on one level), and three new maps: Temple (Ancient), Canal (Anubis) and Park (Overpass).
- Fair starts on every map. The two spawns can't see each other at round start, or within the first 1.5 seconds of running.
- A new radar in the CS overview style:
  - A dark grid with letters and numbers, floors tinted by side, orange site boxes, green spawns and callout names.
  - The name of the spot you're in shows under the radar.
  - Hold Tab to see the whole map. The radar zooms out while you run.

## Smoke, fire and grenades
- Smoke is a real volume. You can't see through it from outside or out of it from inside, and the same rule applies to everyone, including bots and the radar.
- An HE grenade blows a short-lived hole in a smoke, at the same place and moment for every player.
- A smoke puts out a burning molotov. A molotov thrown into smoke fizzles.

## Team voice and chat
- Push-to-talk team voice (K) between team mates only, with speaking icons on the scoreboard.
- Per-player mute, microphone and speaker choice, volume levels and a microphone test (Settings → Team voice).
- The desktop app uses voice right away. Browser players open the host's https address (shown in the invite) and accept its certificate once.
- Quick radio (Z) with calls for each side. Your current callout is attached, and some calls mark your position on your team's radar.
- All chat (Y) and team chat (U), showing who will read the message before you send it. During a live round, the dead only chat with the dead.

## Host controls
- Warmup and freeze time in seconds, including 0.
- Pause and resume the match, restart the round, move a player to spectator, and choose the map for the next match.

## Match report
- When a Defuse match ends, a report shows the result, MVP, kills, deaths and assists, damage per round, accuracy, head shots, utility damage, enemies flashed, plants, defuses and a round-by-round timeline.
- It stays available from the team menu for the rest of the session.

## Weapons and play
- No two guns look alike. The SSG 08 is black with a slim scope; the AWP is bulky and olive.
- Walking over a dropped gun fills an empty slot, keeping its ammo.
- Dead bodies no longer block movement.
- Other players visibly reload, throw, and kneel to plant or defuse.

## Sound
- Volume sliders for master, game effects, team voice, music and interface (Settings → Audio).
- Footsteps and reloads fade out over short distances. Gunfire carries far but gets quieter and duller with distance.
- Walls muffle the sounds behind them.

## Fixes
- With freeze time 0, the host is no longer benched for the first round.
- Smoother frames: less work per frame, and smoke is cheaper to draw while you're inside it.
